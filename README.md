Solara City is an original top-down 2D open-world crime game: a sun-soaked coastal city to explore on foot and by car, with moving traffic, pedestrians, traffic lights and zebra crossings, cars to steal and drive, a 5-star wanted level with pursuing police cruisers, roadblocks and a helicopter, 8 story missions for fixer Marisol Vega, taxi fares, checkpoint races, hidden packages, combat (fists, pistol, shotgun, SMG), a safehouse garage to buy and store cars, three synthesised radio stations, a HUD with a live minimap, a full day/night cycle, synthesised WebAudio sound, and saved progress. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make GTA 6” bounty, $1,666 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. WASD to move/drive, E to enter or exit a car, mouse to aim, click to shoot, Shift for handbrake, Q to switch weapon, R to change radio station, G for the garage (near the green GARAGE lot), P to pause, M to mute. Follow the yellow marker to the next mission.

## What's in v2

- Denser city: 3–6 buildings per block, ~180 trees and palms, benches, hydrants, bus stops, billboards, traffic lights, crosswalks, and 2–5 parked cars per block.
- Real moving traffic (48 cars on the road lines) plus 44 pedestrians, up from a parked-only fleet.
- Garage: buy sports, muscle, truck, beach, taxi or van cars, store up to 3, take them out again.
- Radio: three stations (SOLARA FM, BAY BEATS, NIGHT DRIVE) with their own synth loops; R cycles.
- SMG as a fourth weapon; combat split into its own module alongside vehicles, crowd, police, missions and garage.
