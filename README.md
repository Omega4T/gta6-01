Solara City is an original open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA, built for the make.fun gta6-01 bounty. A sun-soaked coastal city, fully procedural from a fixed seed: drive or walk the streets, steal any car, take eight story jobs from the fixer Marisol Vega, run taxi fares and street races, collect twelve hidden packages, buy cars for the safehouse garage, and outrun the SCPD from zero to five wanted stars — with a helicopter at four. Everything is hand-written in plain JavaScript on a 2D canvas: the city, the traffic, the pedestrians, the police, the combat, the HUD with its live minimap, and all sound and music synthesised with WebAudio. No libraries, no network requests at runtime.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. WASD to walk and drive, mouse to aim, click to shoot, E to enter a car, M to call Marisol for the next job, R to change the radio station, T for taxi work, G at the garage lot, P to pause.
