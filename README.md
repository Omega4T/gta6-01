Solara City is an original top-down 2D open-world crime game: a sun-soaked coastal city to explore on foot and by car, with traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal and drive, a 5-star wanted level with pursuing police cruisers and a helicopter, 8 story missions for fixer Marisol Vega, taxi fares, checkpoint races, hidden packages, combat, a HUD with a live minimap, day/night cycle, synthesised WebAudio sound, and saved progress. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make GTA 6” bounty, $1,666 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. WASD to move/drive, E to enter or exit a car, mouse to aim, click to shoot, Shift for handbrake, Q to switch weapon, P to pause, M to mute. Follow the yellow marker to the next mission.
