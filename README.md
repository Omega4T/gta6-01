Solara City 3D is an original open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA, built for the make.fun gta6-01 bounty — a full 3D rebuild. A sun-soaked coastal city with six districts, rendered in low-poly three.js: drive or walk the streets, steal any car, take eight multi-stage story jobs from the fixer Marisol Vega, run taxi fares and street races, collect twelve hidden packages, buy cars for the safehouse garage, and outrun the SCPD from zero to five wanted stars — with roadblocks at three and the AIR-1 helicopter and its spotlight at four. Everything is hand-written and procedural from a fixed seed: the city, the traffic, the pedestrians, the police, the combat, the HUD with its live minimap, and all sound and music synthesised with WebAudio. three.js r160 is vendored locally under game/vendor; no network requests at runtime.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. WASD to walk and drive, mouse to look and aim, click to shoot, E to enter or steal a car, M to call Marisol for the next job, T for taxi work, G at the green garage lot, Z/X/C for crew abilities, R to change the radio station, P to pause and see career stats.
